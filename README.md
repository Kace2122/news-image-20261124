A one-page newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's
world news in a single 1024x1024 image, drawn with Python (PIL) — every
headline set as real text, so the day's news reads from the image alone.

## The stories on the page

1. **HUANG IS BACK** — Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia chief; shares soar
   30%, the biggest one-day gain since 2003. Trump claims credit for the
   comeback.
2. **SEARCH GOES ON** — 24 dead, about 30 still missing as rescuers keep
   digging at the burned candy factory in Cikande, Banten, Indonesia.
3. **UK SANCTIONS GENERALS** — Britain sanctions Myanmar's top military
   leaders after the Rohingya genocide ruling.
4. **TITLE DEFENCE BEGINS** — World chess champion Gukesh opens his title
   defence against Sindarov in Geneva.
5. **TALKS ON THE BORDER** — Indian and Chinese generals meet in Arunachal
   Pradesh to ease tensions.
6. **TONIGHT: A SUPERMOON MEETS THE SEVEN SISTERS** — the year's brightest
   Moon passes the Pleiades.
7. Also today: Modi opens an AI centre at IIT Patna; Democrats unveil
   their 2028 debate plan.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p /output && python3 main.py
```

`main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` byte-for-byte to
`/output/entry.png` (standard library only, no network, deterministic).
